Before you start

  • These exams help you pinpoint where to spend more time fixing knowledge gaps.
  • The questions are AI generated from the lecture slides. Faculty are the experts and likely shared more in lecture than the slides show.
  • Review your class notes and talk with faculty about any topic you are unsure about.
  • Use this as one tool for planning your study time, not the only way you prepare for your final exam.
